# 🔧 Fix-It Scout

**Learn basic repair, maintenance, and problem-solving skills around the home.**

## Why This Badge Matters
The Fix-It Scout badge teaches you how to handle everyday problems and take care of things. From tools to maintenance, these are the skills that make you a go-to problem-solver.

---

**XP available:** 70 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 11 XP
1. Name 5 tools and what they’re used for. — **2 XP**
2. Help fix or tighten something simple with an adult (screw, battery, etc.). — **5 XP**
3. Check and replace batteries in a flashlight or remote. — **2 XP**
4. Learn how to properly use a flashlight, tape measure, or broom. — **2 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 22 XP
1. Learn how to safely hammer a nail or turn a screwdriver. — **5 XP**
2. Help patch, touch up, or repair something broken at home. — **5 XP**
3. Make a mini tool kit or drawer with home repair supplies. — **5 XP**
4. Learn how to check for burned-out light bulbs and replace them. — **2 XP**
5. Create a checklist of items to inspect and maintain monthly (batteries, filters, lights). — **5 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 37 XP
1. Independently complete 3 small fix-it tasks around the house. — **10 XP**
2. Teach someone else how to safely use basic tools. — **10 XP**
3. Learn about safety gear and when to use it (gloves, goggles, etc.). — **2 XP**
4. Help plan or do a family home improvement project. — **10 XP**
5. Write or share what you’ve learned about fixing and maintaining things. — **5 XP**

## ⚡ Epic Quests
*Optional stretch challenges for bonus XP. Each one can be earned once, at any time.*
