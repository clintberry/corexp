# 🛑 Safety Scout

**Be prepared for emergencies and learn how to keep yourself and others safe.**

## Why This Badge Matters
The Safety Scout badge helps you stay safe at home and in your community. You’ll learn how to prepare for emergencies, prevent accidents, and make smart choices when things go wrong.

---

**XP available:** 65 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 11 XP
1. Learn and recite your full name, address, and a parent’s phone number. — **2 XP**
2. Identify and draw a fire escape route from your home. — **5 XP**
3. Practice how to stop, drop, and roll and what to do in a fire. — **2 XP**
4. Talk about what to do if you get lost in a store or public place. — **2 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 19 XP
1. Build or check a family emergency kit with an adult. — **5 XP**
2. Learn about safe street crossing and bike rules. — **2 XP**
3. Create a personal safety plan (who to call, where to go, etc.). — **5 XP**
4. Practice what to say if a stranger approaches or asks for help. — **5 XP**
5. Help test smoke alarms or check home safety items with an adult. — **2 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 35 XP
1. Create a family emergency binder or plan (contacts, meeting place, etc.). — **10 XP**
2. Teach siblings or friends one safety routine you’ve learned. — **10 XP**
3. Roleplay 3 different emergency scenarios and your response. — **5 XP**
4. Learn how to shut off water or power to your home (with supervision). — **5 XP**
5. Reflect on how being prepared helps you feel confident and calm. — **5 XP**

## ⚡ Epic Quests
*Optional stretch challenges for bonus XP. Each one can be earned once, at any time.*

| Tier | Epic Quest | XP |
|---|---|---|
| ★ Challenger | Plan and lead a timed family fire drill, then improve the time on a second drill. | **15 XP** |
| ★★ Champion | Ride your bike to a destination using every hand signal and road rule while an adult follows. | **25 XP** |
| ★★★ Legend | Pass a swim safety test: tread water for 2 minutes and swim 100 yards without stopping. | **40 XP** |
