# 💸 Money Smart

**Understand the basics of earning, saving, spending, and giving.**

## Why This Badge Matters
The Money Smart badge helps you understand the basics of how to earn, save, and spend money wisely. These are the tools that help you become a responsible and generous steward.

---

**XP available:** 86 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 11 XP
1. Identify 3 ways people earn money. — **2 XP**
2. Sort coins and bills and name their values. — **2 XP**
3. Save money in a piggy bank or envelope for 2 weeks. — **5 XP**
4. Talk about something you bought or want and how you could earn for it. — **2 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 25 XP
1. Create 3 spending categories: save, spend, and give. — **5 XP**
2. Do a small job to earn money and record what you earned. — **5 XP**
3. Plan a budget for a $10–$20 project or goal. — **5 XP**
4. Learn what tithing is (or giving) and give from your own money. — **5 XP**
5. Visit a store and compare prices or read product labels with a parent. — **5 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 50 XP
1. Track your money for 30 days: what you earned, spent, saved, and gave. — **20 XP**
2. Plan and make a larger purchase using saved money. — **10 XP**
3. Teach someone how to use the save/spend/give method. — **10 XP**
4. Research and explain how banks or digital money tools work. — **5 XP**
5. Reflect on a financial decision you made—what went well or what you’d change. — **5 XP**
