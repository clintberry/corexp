# ❤️‍🩹 Bounce Back

**Build resilience and grit when things go wrong.**

## Why This Badge Matters
The Bounce Back badge teaches you how to be resilient—how to get up when life knocks you down. You’ll learn how to face challenges, handle failure, and bounce back stronger with courage and creativity.

---

**XP available:** 80 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 11 XP
1. Name a time something was hard but you kept trying. — **2 XP**
2. Learn what 'resilience' means and why it’s important. — **2 XP**
3. Draw or describe something that helped you feel better after disappointment. — **5 XP**
4. Talk about someone you admire who is resilient and what they did. — **2 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 19 XP
1. Write about a time you made a mistake and recovered from it. — **5 XP**
2. Learn and explain the difference between problem-solving and giving up. — **2 XP**
3. Try a difficult task 3 times and track your progress. — **5 XP**
4. Practice asking for help in a real situation. — **2 XP**
5. Create a resilience checklist with strategies that help you bounce back. — **5 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 50 XP
1. Set a meaningful goal that challenges you and work on it for 30 days. — **20 XP**
2. Reflect on 3 personal setbacks and what you learned from them. — **5 XP**
3. Interview someone about a time they bounced back from something hard. — **10 XP**
4. Help a friend or sibling through a difficult situation with empathy. — **5 XP**
5. Create a short presentation, poster, or video about bouncing back from failure. — **10 XP**

## ⚡ Epic Quests
*Optional stretch challenges for bonus XP. Each one can be earned once, at any time.*

| Tier | Epic Quest | XP |
|---|---|---|
| ★ Challenger | Try out, audition, or compete in something you're afraid of failing at, then write about how it went. | **15 XP** |
| ★★ Champion | Learn a skill that takes lots of failed attempts (juggling 3 balls, solving a Rubik's cube, a skateboard trick) and log every attempt until you get it. | **25 XP** |
| ★★★ Legend | Go back to a goal you once failed at and achieve it. | **40 XP** |
