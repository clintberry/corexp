# 🧘 Stillness Scout

**Practice mindfulness, breathing, and being present.**

## Why This Badge Matters
The Stillness Scout badge helps you practice mindfulness and become more present. You’ll learn how to pause, notice what’s happening around and inside you, and respond with peace and focus instead of stress or distraction.

---

**XP available:** 94 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 14 XP
1. Try a 2-minute guided breathing exercise 3 times. — **5 XP**
2. Sit quietly and notice 5 things you can see, hear, or feel. — **2 XP**
3. Draw a picture of a peaceful place or moment. — **5 XP**
4. Notice how your body feels before and after a calming break. — **2 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 25 XP
1. Practice mindfulness for 5 minutes a day for 5 days. — **5 XP**
2. Learn and try 3 types of mindfulness (breathing, body scan, mindful eating). — **5 XP**
3. Take a 20-minute walk and observe your surroundings silently. — **5 XP**
4. Reflect on how mindfulness helped you in a stressful moment. — **5 XP**
5. Create a short mindfulness routine for mornings or evenings. — **5 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 55 XP
1. Complete a 14-day mindfulness challenge (5+ min/day). — **20 XP**
2. Teach or guide someone else through a mindfulness exercise. — **10 XP**
3. Learn and explain how mindfulness affects the brain and body. — **5 XP**
4. Keep a log of what you notice during mindfulness over 2 weeks. — **10 XP**
5. Create a mindfulness guide or video for other kids or family members. — **10 XP**

## ⚡ Epic Quests
*Optional stretch challenges for bonus XP. Each one can be earned once, at any time.*
