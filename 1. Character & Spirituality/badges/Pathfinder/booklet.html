About this badgeWhy This Badge Matters

The Pathfinder badge helps you discover and live by the values that define who you are and who you want to become. Values like honesty, courage, faith, and kindness are more than words—they guide our choices and shape our character.

As you explore your beliefs and put them into practice, you'll build integrity and strength from the inside out. This badge encourages you to be intentional about how you live and to become someone others can trust and look up to.

How to use this booklet

  1. Work through the levels in order: Explorer, then Builder, then Master.
  2. Record what you did in the space under each requirement.
  3. When you finish one, write the date and ask your mentor to initial it.
  4. Color in the circle below for every requirement you finish.
