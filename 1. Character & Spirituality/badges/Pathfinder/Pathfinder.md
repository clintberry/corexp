# 🛐 Pathfinder

**Exploring and living by your values.**

## Why This Badge Matters
The Pathfinder badge helps you discover and live by the values that define who you are and who you want to become. Values like honesty, courage, faith, and kindness are more than words—they guide our choices and shape our character. As you explore your beliefs and put them into practice, you'll build integrity and strength from the inside out. This badge encourages you to be intentional about how you live and to become someone others can trust and look up to.

---

**XP available:** 89 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 14 XP
1. Identify 3 core personal or family values. — **2 XP**
2. Write or tell someone why each value is important to you. — **5 XP**
3. Share a time when you saw someone live one of those values. — **2 XP**
4. Memorize a quote or scripture that represents one of your chosen values. — **5 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 35 XP
1. Keep a 7-day journal reflecting on how you lived your values. — **10 XP**
2. Identify a time you were tempted to go against a value. What did you do? — **5 XP**
3. Interview a parent or mentor about their values. — **10 XP**
4. Discuss values shown or violated in a movie or story. — **5 XP**
5. Create a symbol, art, or written expression of a value. — **5 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 40 XP
1. Choose one core value and live it deeply for 30 days. — **20 XP**
2. Lead a discussion or presentation about values. — **10 XP**
3. Reflect on a decision or relationship shaped by your values. — **5 XP**
4. Apply a value to a real-world issue and explain your response. — **5 XP**

## ⚡ Epic Quests
*Optional stretch challenges for bonus XP. Each one can be earned once, at any time.*

| Tier | Epic Quest | XP |
|---|---|---|
| ★ Challenger | Memorize 5 quotes or scriptures, one for each of 5 different values. | **15 XP** |
