Mission briefingWhy This Badge Matters

The Kindness Agent badge is about using small, simple acts to make a big difference. Whether you're helping a neighbor, cheering up a sibling, or showing gratitude, kindness is a superpower that strengthens families, friendships, and communities.

This badge helps you practice empathy, look for ways to serve, and recognize the impact of your actions. The goal is to become someone who sees needs—and acts—with love and compassion.

How to use this mission log

  1. Complete your missions in order: Explorer, then Builder, then Master.
  2. Record what you did in the space under each mission.
  3. When you finish one, write the date and ask your mentor to initial it.
  4. Color in the circle below for every mission you finish.
