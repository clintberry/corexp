# 🙏 Spiritual Seeker (LDS Focus)

**Growing in your relationship with Heavenly Father, Jesus Christ, and the Holy Ghost.**

## Why This Badge Matters
The Spiritual Seeker badge invites you to grow closer to Heavenly Father, strengthen your faith in Jesus Christ, and learn how the Holy Ghost can guide your life. As you complete this badge, you'll practice meaningful spiritual habits like prayer, scripture study, and service. These experiences will help you understand your divine identity, build your personal testimony, and prepare you to make and keep sacred covenants. The goal is not just to check off tasks, but to discover how living the gospel brings peace, purpose, and joy.

---

**XP available:** 125 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 20 XP
1. Pray morning and night for 3 days in a row. — **5 XP**
2. Read scriptures with family or alone for 3 days. — **5 XP**
3. Memorize and recite one Article of Faith. — **5 XP**
4. Attend sacrament meeting and share something you learned or felt. — **5 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 45 XP
1. Study a scripture hero and share what you admire. — **5 XP**
2. Keep a spiritual journal for 7 days. — **10 XP**
3. Choose a Christlike attribute and work on it for a week. — **10 XP**
4. Memorize three Articles of Faith and explain them. — **10 XP**
5. Watch or listen to a General Conference talk and discuss it. — **5 XP**
6. Complete one family history task (add photo/story or interview elder). — **5 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 60 XP
1. Create and follow a 30-day spiritual habit plan. — **20 XP**
2. Give a talk or testimony in a family or church setting. — **10 XP**
3. Create a gospel study project on a chosen topic. — **10 XP**
4. Attend the temple or prepare family names with a parent. — **10 XP**
5. Lead or plan a family home evening lesson. — **10 XP**
