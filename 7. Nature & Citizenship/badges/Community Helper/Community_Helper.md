# 🧹 Community Helper

**Serve your neighborhood or community through small acts that make a difference.**

## Why This Badge Matters
The Community Helper badge encourages you to serve others and make your neighborhood a better place. Even small acts of service can have a big impact on the people around you.

---

**XP available:** 109 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 14 XP
1. Do something kind for a neighbor (e.g., letter, help with yard, say hello). — **2 XP**
2. Help with a community or church clean-up project. — **5 XP**
3. Learn about someone in your community who serves others. — **2 XP**
4. Draw or write about a problem in your community and how you might help. — **5 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 40 XP
1. Collect and donate items for a cause (food, toys, books, etc.). — **10 XP**
2. Volunteer your time with a parent for at least 1 hour. — **10 XP**
3. Plan and complete a service project with your family or group. — **10 XP**
4. Write a thank-you note to someone who helps your community. — **5 XP**
5. Talk with a local leader or community member about what they do. — **5 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 55 XP
1. Lead or organize a service project or campaign (with guidance). — **20 XP**
2. Keep a service journal and reflect on 5 things you’ve done to help others. — **10 XP**
3. Teach or involve others in a community improvement effort. — **10 XP**
4. Research a local need and present an idea to help. — **10 XP**
5. Reflect on how helping others has helped you grow. — **5 XP**

## ⚡ Epic Quests
*Optional stretch challenges for bonus XP. Each one can be earned once, at any time.*

| Tier | Epic Quest | XP |
|---|---|---|
| ★ Challenger | Volunteer 10 hours of service. | **15 XP** |
| ★★ Champion | Volunteer 25 hours of service. | **25 XP** |
| ★★★ Legend | Volunteer 50 hours of service in one year. | **40 XP** |
