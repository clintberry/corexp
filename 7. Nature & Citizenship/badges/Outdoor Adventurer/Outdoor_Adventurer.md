# 🧭 Outdoor Adventurer

**Build confidence and skills in the outdoors—hiking, camping, survival basics.**

## Why This Badge Matters
The Outdoor Adventurer badge helps you enjoy the outdoors safely and confidently. You’ll learn how to prepare for outdoor experiences, explore nature, and take care of yourself outside.

---

**XP available:** 86 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 14 XP
1. Go on a nature walk or hike and observe your surroundings. — **5 XP**
2. Pack a day bag with essentials for an outdoor adventure. — **2 XP**
3. Learn and practice basic navigation (compass or trail map). — **5 XP**
4. Talk about what to do if you get lost in the woods or outside. — **2 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 27 XP
1. Help plan and go on a longer hike or outdoor trip. — **10 XP**
2. Build a basic shelter, knot, or survival tool using natural materials. — **5 XP**
3. Learn how to layer clothing for outdoor conditions. — **2 XP**
4. Practice outdoor cooking or build a safe pretend campfire. — **5 XP**
5. Keep a 5-day log of time spent outdoors and what you did each day. — **5 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 45 XP
1. Plan and complete an outdoor activity that requires preparation and safety (hike, campout, or nature challenge). — **20 XP**
2. Teach an outdoor skill (navigation, fire safety, knot tying) to someone else. — **10 XP**
3. Identify 10 plants or animals native to your region. — **5 XP**
4. Create a poster, video, or guide for first-time hikers or campers. — **5 XP**
5. Reflect on what you’ve learned about being prepared and respectful outdoors. — **5 XP**

## ⚡ Epic Quests
*Optional stretch challenges for bonus XP. Each one can be earned once, at any time.*

|---|---|---|
