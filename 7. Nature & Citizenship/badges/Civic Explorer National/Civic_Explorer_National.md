# 🦅 Civic Explorer – National

**Understand how the U.S. government works and what it means to be a responsible American citizen.**

## Why This Badge Matters

The Civic Explorer – National badge helps you learn how the United States government works and how you can participate as a citizen. You’ll explore the Constitution, national leaders, and how laws and elections shape the country.

---

**XP available:** 73 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 8 XP

1. Learn the three branches of the U.S. government and what each one does. — **2 XP**
2. Name the current President and one member of Congress from your state. — **2 XP**
3. Recite or learn about a line from the Preamble to the Constitution. — **2 XP**
4. Talk about what it means to be a good citizen of the United States. — **2 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 30 XP

1. Track a national issue or current event and talk about it with your family. — **5 XP**
2. Create a poster or slideshow about one U.S. symbol (e.g., flag, eagle, monument). — **5 XP**
3. Simulate an election by researching candidates and voting as a family. — **10 XP**
4. Watch a portion of a Congressional hearing or speech and summarize it. — **5 XP**
5. Learn about a national hero or civil rights leader and their impact. — **5 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 35 XP

1. Write to a U.S. senator or representative about a national issue that matters to you. — **5 XP**
2. Teach someone younger about the Constitution or Bill of Rights. — **10 XP**
3. Create a civics quiz, video, or activity for your family or group. — **10 XP**
4. Reflect on how your voice matters in a democracy and what you want to stand for. — **5 XP**
5. Learn about a landmark U.S. Supreme Court case and explain its significance. — **5 XP**
