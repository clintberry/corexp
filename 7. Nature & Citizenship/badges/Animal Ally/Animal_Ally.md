# 🐾 Animal Ally

**Learn about animal care, kindness to creatures, and respecting wildlife.**

## Why This Badge Matters
The Animal Ally badge teaches you to care for pets and respect animals in nature. You’ll learn what animals need and how to show kindness to all creatures—big and small.

---

**XP available:** 96 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 14 XP
1. Help feed, groom, or clean up after a pet. — **2 XP**
2. Visit or learn about a wild animal at a zoo or sanctuary. — **5 XP**
3. Make a bird feeder or bug habitat and observe it. — **5 XP**
4. Talk about why it’s important to be gentle with animals. — **2 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 32 XP
1. Learn about a local animal rescue or shelter and what they do. — **2 XP**
2. Create a care guide or checklist for a pet or animal you know. — **5 XP**
3. Keep a log of how you care for a pet or animal for 1 week. — **10 XP**
4. Make a donation or volunteer with an adult at an animal-related group. — **10 XP**
5. Research how to safely observe wild animals in your area. — **5 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 50 XP
1. Teach others about responsible pet ownership or wildlife safety. — **10 XP**
2. Create a video, poster, or story about an animal you care about. — **5 XP**
3. Design an awareness or fundraising effort for animals in need. — **20 XP**
4. Interview someone who works with animals and share what you learned. — **10 XP**
5. Reflect on how your actions can help animals and ecosystems. — **5 XP**

## ⚡ Epic Quests
*Optional stretch challenges for bonus XP. Each one can be earned once, at any time.*

| Tier | Epic Quest | XP |
|---|---|---|
| ★ Challenger | Take full responsibility for a pet's care for 2 weeks with no reminders. | **15 XP** |
| ★★ Champion | Teach a pet 3 new commands or tricks. | **25 XP** |
