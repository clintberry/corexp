# 🫶 Empathy & Friendship

**Understand how others feel and respond with kindness and connection.**

## Why This Badge Matters
The Empathy & Friendship badge teaches you how to understand others' feelings and build strong, respectful relationships. By practicing kindness, compassion, and connection, you’ll grow into someone who lifts others up and creates real friendships.

---

**XP available:** 99 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 14 XP
1. Name at least 4 different feelings someone else might have. — **2 XP**
2. Do 3 kind things for friends or family and don't tell them you did it. — **5 XP**
3. Listen to someone talk about their day and ask 2 follow-up questions. — **2 XP**
4. Draw or describe what a good friend does and how they make others feel. — **5 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 35 XP
1. Interview someone about a time they felt misunderstood and what helped. — **10 XP**
2. Make a plan to include someone who might feel left out (at school, church, or family). — **10 XP**
3. Notice how someone else is feeling and try to help or support them. — **5 XP**
4. Keep a kindness log for 5 days and reflect on how it felt to serve others. — **5 XP**
5. Read a story or watch a video about empathy and share what you learned. — **5 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 50 XP
1. Be a mentor or 'buddy' to someone younger or newer in a group. — **20 XP**
2. Write or create something about a time someone showed you empathy. — **5 XP**
3. Plan and complete a small service project to support a friend or family member. — **10 XP**
4. Teach a sibling or friend about what empathy means and how to show it. — **10 XP**
5. Reflect on how empathy has made a difference in your relationships. — **5 XP**
