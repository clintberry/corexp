# 🤝 Team & Conflict Skills

**Work with others, cooperate in groups, and solve conflicts peacefully.**

## Why This Badge Matters
The Team & Conflict Skills badge helps you learn how to cooperate, share ideas, and solve disagreements in peaceful ways. Being a great teammate and peacemaker helps groups succeed and makes you a stronger leader.

---

**XP available:** 83 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 11 XP
1. Join a group activity and take turns or share responsibilities. — **5 XP**
2. Practice saying, 'Let’s work together' or 'What do you think?' in a group. — **2 XP**
3. Talk about what makes someone a good teammate. — **2 XP**
4. Describe a time when you disagreed with someone and how it got worked out. — **2 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 27 XP
1. Practice using calm words and listening during a disagreement. — **5 XP**
2. Roleplay solving a conflict using steps: listen, share, find a solution. — **5 XP**
3. Join or lead a group project where everyone contributes something. — **10 XP**
4. Talk about how being bossy or passive affects a group—and how to find balance. — **2 XP**
5. Reflect on a real disagreement and write or talk about what you could do better next time. — **5 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 45 XP
1. Lead a team activity and help the group stay respectful and fair. — **10 XP**
2. Teach someone else about how to solve a conflict with words, not anger. — **10 XP**
3. Keep a journal for 10 days tracking your teamwork or leadership experiences. — **10 XP**
4. Research and present a peacemaking technique (like compromise or mediation). — **10 XP**
5. Create a guide or poster showing the steps for resolving conflict kindly. — **5 XP**
