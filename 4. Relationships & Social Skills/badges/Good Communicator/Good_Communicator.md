# 🗣️ Good Communicator

**Learn to speak clearly, listen actively, and express yourself kindly.**

## Why This Badge Matters
The Good Communicator badge helps you express yourself clearly and kindly, while also learning how to really listen. Whether you're in a conversation, a group, or solving a problem, strong communication helps you be heard and build trust.

---

**XP available:** 75 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 11 XP
1. Practice active listening in 3 conversations (look, nod, don't interrupt). — **5 XP**
2. Say something kind or encouraging to 3 people. — **2 XP**
3. Tell a short story or explain something you know to a family member. — **2 XP**
4. Learn about body language and name 3 things people can show without words. — **2 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 24 XP
1. Write or say how you feel using 'I' statements in a real situation. — **2 XP**
2. Practice giving and receiving feedback respectfully. — **5 XP**
3. Prepare and give a short talk, story, or presentation to your family or group. — **10 XP**
4. Roleplay introducing yourself or making small talk with someone new. — **5 XP**
5. Learn how tone and facial expressions change the meaning of your words. — **2 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 40 XP
1. Lead or co-lead a group conversation or discussion (family, group, class). — **10 XP**
2. Write or record a reflection on what makes a great communicator. — **5 XP**
3. Teach someone how to use 'I' statements or active listening. — **10 XP**
4. Keep a log of 3 difficult conversations and what you learned from each. — **10 XP**
5. Research a famous communicator and share what made them effective. — **5 XP**
