# 🏅 CoreXP Awards

Beyond the 34 badges, CoreXP has three **level patches** and one top award, the **Winged Lion Award** medal. Each level patch takes the required badges at that level, some electives, and three Epic Quests of the matching tier. The top award takes the Master patch and a major service project.

---

## Level Patches

| Patch | Badges at | Epic Quests |
|---|---|---|
| 🥉 Explorer | Explorer level: 22 required + 6 electives | 3 ★ Challenger quests |
| 🥈 Builder | Builder level: 22 required + 6 electives | 3 ★★ Champion quests |
| 🥇 Master | Master level: 22 required + 6 electives | 3 ★★★ Legend quests |

The Epic Quests can come from any badges and can be done at any time, before or after the level requirements.

### Required and elective badges

Each level patch takes all **22 required badges** at that level, plus **6 electives** at that level, with at least **2** from Creativity & Innovation. A badge counts toward a patch once that level of it is done, so the electives can be different at each level.

| Category | Required | Electives |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Character & Spirituality | Kindness Agent, Pathfinder, Spiritual Seeker (LDS Focus) | — |
| 2. Health & Physical Wellness | Clean & Capable, First Aid Hero, Fit Force, Fuel Master | Rest & Recharge |
| 3. Emotional & Mental Resilience | Bounce Back, Calm Commander, Self-Reflector | Mind Master, Stillness Scout |
| 4. Relationships & Social Skills | Empathy & Friendship, Good Communicator, Team & Conflict Skills | — |
| 5. Practical Life Skills | Home Helper, Money Smart, Personal Manager, Safety Scout | Fix-It Scout |
| 6. Creativity & Innovation | — | Creative Maker, Digital Creator, Idea Builder, Music Explorer, Storyteller |
| 7. Nature & Citizenship | Civic Explorer – Local, Civic Explorer – State, Civic Explorer – National, Civic Explorer – World, Community Helper | Animal Ally, Earth Steward, Outdoor Adventurer |

Community Helper is required because it's practice for the Winged Lion service project.

## 🦁 Winged Lion Award

**The highest award in CoreXP, named for our family mascot: lead a service project that leaves something better than you found it.**

The award is a medal rather than a patch, in the tradition of the Eagle Scout medal and the Lion of Saint Mark: a bronze winged lion striding across an open medallion, framed by laurel and oak leaves, hung from a crimson ribbon edged in navy and gold. The shield at the bottom has room to engrave the recipient's initials and the year. Artwork: [`awards/winged-lion-medal.jpg`](awards/winged-lion-medal.jpg). There's also a **Winged Lion patch** to go with it, for the banner above the level patches: a gold winged lion framed by laurel and oak leaves, ringed by the family values HONEST · GRATEFUL · DILIGENT · BRAVE · FUN. Artwork: [`awards/winged-lion-patch.jpg`](awards/winged-lion-patch.jpg).

Prerequisite: earn the **Master** level patch.

### Requirements

1. Find a real need in your community, church, or school, and choose a project that meets it. Talk with the person or group it will help.
2. Write a project proposal: the need, what you'll do, materials and costs, how you'll get them, who will help, a timeline, and how you'll keep everyone safe.
3. Get the proposal approved by your parents, a mentor from outside the family, and the person or group the project will help.
4. Raise or gather the materials and money you need yourself, without your parents paying for them.
5. Recruit and lead a team of at least 3 helpers. You plan the work and give directions; you don't do it all alone.
6. Carry out the project, with at least 40 hours of total work (your hours plus your helpers').
7. Keep a project log of each work session: the date, who helped, the hours, and what got done.
8. Write a final report with photos: what went well, what changed from the plan, and what you learned about leading.
9. Thank everyone who helped, in person or in writing.
10. Meet with a Winged Lion review panel (your parents and your outside mentor) to present the project and talk about your CoreXP journey and what's next.
