# 🎨 Creative Maker

**Express yourself through art, crafts, design, or building projects.**

## Why This Badge Matters
The Creative Maker badge lets you explore your imagination through hands-on projects. From drawing and painting to building and crafting, this badge encourages you to create something original with your own hands.

---

**XP available:** 96 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 17 XP
1. Make something creative using paper, crayons, or markers. — **5 XP**
2. Try a craft or DIY project with a parent or sibling. — **5 XP**
3. Visit an art museum (in person or virtual) and talk about what you liked. — **5 XP**
4. Describe or draw your idea for a fun invention or product. — **2 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 32 XP
1. Create a finished art or design project using at least 3 materials. — **10 XP**
2. Learn about and try a new type of visual art (e.g., clay, collage, paint). — **5 XP**
3. Enter a project in a fair, show, or display it at home. — **5 XP**
4. Teach a younger child how to do a creative project. — **10 XP**
5. Write or talk about what inspires your creativity. — **2 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 47 XP
1. Complete a multi-day creative project (e.g., sculpture, diorama, design). — **20 XP**
2. Create a collection or portfolio of your work (digital or physical). — **10 XP**
3. Design something useful or beautiful for your home or family. — **10 XP**
4. Learn and explain one element or principle of design (e.g., balance, color). — **2 XP**
5. Reflect on how your creativity has grown and changed. — **5 XP**

## ⚡ Epic Quests
*Optional stretch challenges for bonus XP. Each one can be earned once, at any time.*

| Tier | Epic Quest | XP |
|---|---|---|
| ★ Challenger | Do 30 sketches in 30 days. | **15 XP** |
| ★★ Champion | Sell or give away 5 of your handmade creations. | **25 XP** |
| ★★★ Legend | Host an art show of at least 10 of your pieces for family and friends. | **40 XP** |
