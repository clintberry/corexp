# 🎶 Music Explorer

**Explore rhythm, sound, instruments, or voice as creative expression.**

## Why This Badge Matters
The Music Explorer badge helps you connect with rhythm, melody, and sound. Whether you're singing, drumming, or just listening closely, music is a powerful way to express emotion and creativity.

---

**XP available:** 137 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 11 XP
1. Listen to 3 songs from different styles or cultures and share what you notice. — **2 XP**
2. Try keeping a beat with your hands or a homemade drum. — **2 XP**
3. Sing or play along with a favorite song. — **2 XP**
4. Make a simple instrument (like a shaker or rubber band guitar). — **5 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 27 XP
1. Learn and perform a short song (voice or instrument). — **10 XP**
2. Experiment with writing your own rhythm, melody, or lyrics. — **5 XP**
3. Learn about a musical instrument and how it's played. — **2 XP**
4. Play or sing music with someone else. — **5 XP**
5. Create a playlist for a specific mood or event and explain your choices. — **5 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 99 XP
1. Complete a creative music project (recording, composition, performance). — **20 XP**
2. Teach a musical skill to a sibling or friend. — **10 XP**
3. Learn basic music vocabulary (tempo, pitch, rhythm, dynamics). — **2 XP**
4. Attend a live or virtual music event and reflect on what you experienced. — **5 XP**
5. Share how music helps you express emotions or connect with others. — **2 XP**
6. Practice a musical instrument 5 days per week for 12 weeks — **40 XP**
7. Memorize a song on a musical instrument and perform it at a recital or a family event — **20 XP**

## ⚡ Epic Quests
*Optional stretch challenges for bonus XP. Each one can be earned once, at any time.*
