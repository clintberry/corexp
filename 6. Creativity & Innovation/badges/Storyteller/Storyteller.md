# 📝 Storyteller

**Use words, pictures, or video to tell original stories or share experiences.**

## Why This Badge Matters
The Storyteller badge invites you to use words, pictures, or video to share stories. Telling stories helps you connect with others, understand yourself, and bring your ideas to life.

---

**XP available:** 93 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 14 XP
1. Tell a made-up story out loud or through pictures. — **2 XP**
2. Draw or write a short story with a beginning, middle, and end. — **5 XP**
3. Record yourself telling a joke or true story and share it. — **5 XP**
4. Make a list of your favorite books, shows, or storytellers. — **2 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 34 XP
1. Write or illustrate a complete short story or comic. — **10 XP**
2. Share a true story from your life with a parent or group. — **2 XP**
3. Create a short skit or puppet show and perform it. — **10 XP**
4. Try storytelling with a new medium (e.g., stop-motion, audio, slideshow). — **10 XP**
5. Give constructive feedback on someone else's story or performance. — **2 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 45 XP
1. Create and present a longer storytelling project (e.g., book, video, zine). — **20 XP**
2. Enter a story into a contest or publish it (even just for your family). — **5 XP**
3. Teach someone how to build a story with characters and plot. — **10 XP**
4. Write about what you’ve learned from telling or hearing stories. — **5 XP**
5. Reflect on how storytelling helps you connect with others. — **5 XP**

## ⚡ Epic Quests
*Optional stretch challenges for bonus XP. Each one can be earned once, at any time.*

| Tier | Epic Quest | XP |
|---|---|---|
| ★ Challenger | Write 7 short stories in 7 days. | **15 XP** |
| ★★ Champion | Read 10 books in one season. | **25 XP** |
| ★★★ Legend | Write and bind (or publish) a book of at least 20 pages. | **40 XP** |
