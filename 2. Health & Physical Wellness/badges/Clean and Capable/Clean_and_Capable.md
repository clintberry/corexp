# 🧼 Clean & Capable

**Learn to take care of your hygiene, grooming, and personal environment so you stay clean, healthy, and confident.**

## Why This Badge Matters
The Clean & Capable badge helps you take responsibility for your body and your space. By learning how to keep yourself clean and your surroundings tidy, you’ll feel more confident, respectful, and ready to thrive. This badge teaches the everyday habits that make a big difference in how you feel and how others see you.

---

**XP available:** 111 XP from requirements · up to 80 more from Epic Quests

## Explorer (Level 1) · 17 XP
1. Brush your teeth and floss daily for 5 days in a row. — **5 XP**
2. Learn and demonstrate proper handwashing. — **2 XP**
3. Practice showering or bathing regularly (at least 3 times/week). — **5 XP**
4. Keep your bedroom clean for 3 days in a row. — **5 XP**

## Builder (Level 2) · 34 XP
1. Create a morning and evening hygiene routine and follow it for 7 days. — **10 XP**
2. Learn how to properly clip fingernails and toenails. — **2 XP**
3. Sort, wash, and put away your own laundry at least twice. — **5 XP**
4. Keep your bedroom clean and organized for 7 days. — **10 XP**
5. Talk with a parent about body changes and hygiene needs as you grow. — **2 XP**
6. Help clean a shared space in your home (bathroom, kitchen, etc.). — **5 XP**

## Master (Level 3) · 60 XP
1. Follow your hygiene routine consistently for 30 days. — **20 XP**
2. Teach a sibling or friend how to care for their hygiene or space. — **10 XP**
3. Research and explain how hygiene affects self-confidence and health. — **5 XP**
4. Create a hygiene checklist for yourself or others to use. — **5 XP**
5. Keep your room and one shared space clean and organized for 2 weeks. — **20 XP**
